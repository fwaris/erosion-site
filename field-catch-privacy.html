    Field Catch Privacy Policy¶

    Effective November 27, 2026 · Field Catch for iPhone and iPad

    Field Catch lets two people play together without giving us a name, email address, or phone number. We don’t keep a history of your matches, record your calls, sell personal information, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

    When you create or join a field¶

    Field Catch uses Google Firebase to introduce your device to your partner’s. Firebase assigns an anonymous player identifier. Its Realtime Database stores the field code, the two players’ identifiers, the field’s expiration time, and the technical messages the devices exchange to find each other. These messages can include network addresses. Your device also contacts Google’s STUN service to find its public network address. A direct connection can reveal that address to your partner.

    A field expires after about an hour. This ends player access to its signaling records. The records can remain in Firebase until the developer removes them. Firebase also processes technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its services. Google describes its retention periods in its Firebase privacy information.

    What your partner receives¶

    During a match, game updates travel directly between the two devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection. When you tap Ready to catch and allow location access, your phone shares precise position readings with your partner so both phones can place the ball and score the catch.

    Your microphone starts muted. Unmuting shares your voice with your partner. A video call uses the camera with your permission. View in AR uses the rear camera on your device. Share AR lets your partner see that view. Firebase does not receive your gameplay location readings, scores, microphone audio, or camera stream.

    Maps, storage, and your choices¶

    Field Catch uses Apple Maps to display the playing field. Loading the map can send requests to Apple for the area shown. The app remembers the last map center and zoom on your device. Versions that ask you to agree before playing also store the accepted terms version locally. Field Catch EULA and player terms Apple’s privacy policy

    You can mute the microphone, stop sharing AR, end a call, or leave the field. Leaving closes the peer connection. You can revoke location, camera, or microphone access at any time in your device’s Settings → Privacy & Security. Deleting the app removes its local settings.

    Contact¶

    For a privacy question or a request about a Firebase field record, contact Faisal Waris through the Field Catch website’s issue tracker. That page is public. Please describe your request in general terms and keep field codes, locations, and other personal details out of the post.
