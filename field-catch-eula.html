    Field Catch EULA and player terms¶

    Effective November 27, 2026

    Walk to play. Stop moving before looking at the map or through the camera. Play in daylight or good visibility, on level ground in an open area where you have permission to be.

    Skip any unsafe catch¶

    A virtual ball can land across a road, near water, on private property, or beyond a fence. GPS can be wrong. Check your surroundings yourself. Never cross traffic, climb, trespass, or run to beat the timer. Tap “Skip unsafe catch” to retry the turn. Both players keep their scores.

    Playing with someone else¶

    Share the field code with someone you trust. During play your partner can receive your location. Unmute your microphone or share a camera view only when you want your partner to hear or see you. Respect other people’s privacy and get permission before showing them on camera.

    Agreement to play¶

    These terms are between you and Faisal Waris, the developer of Field Catch. By choosing “I agree,” you agree to these rules and acknowledge that moving outdoors can involve falls, collisions, or other injuries. You can leave the field at any time. These terms preserve your rights under applicable law, including rights relating to personal injury.
